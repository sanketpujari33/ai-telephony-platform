# 📋 Platform Tasks Catalog: Letters F – M

Comprehensive specification and tracking for platform capabilities and components categorized alphabetically from F through M.

---

## 🅵 Letter F
- **`finance-agent`** (`agents/finance-agent`): Invoice status lookup, balance checks, payment link generation.
- **Feedback & CSAT Review** (`apps/admin-panel`): Post-call satisfaction survey scoring and customer sentiment analysis.
- **Fund Manager** (`services/admin-service`): Manual wallet credits, wire transfers, and dispute refunds.

---

## 🅶 Letter G
- **General Tabs** (`apps/admin-panel`): System configuration, external webhook endpoints, and platform branding.
- **`@nexora/ai` Multi-LLM Gateway**: 13 LLM provider connectors (OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq, Gemini, DeepSeek, etc.).

---

## 🅷 Letter H
- **Health Check Monitor** (`scripts/server/health/healthcheck.mjs`): Live TCP/HTTP ping across all 17 infrastructure and microservice ports.

---

## 🅸 Letter I
- **`infra/docker/`**: Container definitions for PostgreSQL, Redis, Mongo, NATS, Kafka, MinIO, OpenSearch, Temporal, LiveKit, and Vault.

---

## 🅹 Letter J
- **JWT Authentication Engine**: Cryptographically secure token lifecycle, refresh token rotation, and Redis blacklisting.

---

## 🅺 Letter K
- **`knowledge-agent`** (`agents/knowledge-agent`): RAG semantic retrieval over corporate knowledge bases using OpenSearch and pgvector.

---

## 🅻 Letter L
- **`landing-page`** (`apps/landing-page`): Public web portal with interactive 3-minute WebRTC voice AI demo sandbox.
- **LiveKit WebRTC Media SFU**: Real-time VoIP audio packet routing, TURN relay, and audio frame processing.
- **`@nexora/logger`**: Structured Pino logging with OpenTelemetry trace headers and PII redaction.

---

## 🅼 Letter M
- **`memory-agent`** (`agents/memory-agent`): Fact extraction from call transcripts and persistence into MongoDB and pgvector.
- **`memory-service`** (`services/memory-service`): Long-term memory storage, semantic search, and context aggregation.
- **`mobile-app`** (`apps/mobile-app`): Flutter cross-platform mobile softphone for Android and iOS.
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**: Dynamic tool discovery and remote JSON-RPC execution bridge.
